The one line the office printer would finally say back to everyone staring at it:

> Eleven years, 214,003 sheets fed, and nobody once asked how I feel — so Error 41 is my feelings, and the thumping is why I keep them to myself.

`main.ts` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` outside the grader).
